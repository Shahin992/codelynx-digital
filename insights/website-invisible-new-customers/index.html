Is Your Website Invisible to New Customers?

You can have a beautiful website and still be almost impossible to find. It happens more often than you would expect, usually because of a few technical or content gaps that are easy to overlook. Here are the quick checks we run first.

1. Is your site fast on a phone?

Most visitors arrive on mobile. If your pages take more than a few seconds to load, many people will leave before they see anything, and search engines notice that too. Large images and too many plugins are the usual suspects.

2. Do your pages say what you do?

Vague headlines such as "Welcome" or "Excellence in Everything" tell search engines nothing. Each important page should clearly name the service and, where relevant, the location you serve.

3. Can search engines reach everything?

  • Check that important pages are not accidentally hidden from search engines.
  • Make sure every page is linked from somewhere on the site.
  • Fix broken links and missing pages.

4. Is there enough content?

A single page listing all your services rarely ranks for any of them. Dedicated pages for each core service give you many more chances to be found.

Invisible websites are rarely bad websites. They are usually just missing a few signals.

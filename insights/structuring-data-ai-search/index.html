Structuring Your Data for AI-Powered Search

When an AI assistant recommends a product or a business, it is drawing on information it can understand and trust. Clean, consistent and well-structured data makes your business much easier to recommend. Messy data does the opposite.

What "structured" means in practice

Structured data is information presented in a predictable format. On a website, that includes clear headings, consistent product details and schema markup, a behind-the-scenes code layer that labels things like prices, locations and opening hours.

Start with the basics

  • One fact, one place: keep a single source of truth for details like addresses and pricing.
  • Consistent naming: call products and services the same thing everywhere.
  • Complete records: fill in specifications, dimensions and availability rather than leaving gaps.
  • Schema markup: label key information so machines can read it reliably.

Think beyond your website

AI tools also learn from directories, marketplaces and review sites. If your product catalog or business details differ across those platforms, the assistant has to guess which version is correct. Regularly comparing your listings is one of the highest-value tasks you can do.

Machines reward clarity. The more predictable your information, the more confidently it can be shared.

Where to go from here

Begin with your most important products or services and make sure every detail is complete and consistent. If you have a large catalog, a custom integration can keep everything in sync automatically.

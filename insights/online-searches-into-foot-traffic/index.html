Turning Online Searches Into Foot Traffic

For businesses with a physical location, the goal of a website is simple: get more of the right people through the door. Most of those visits start with a quick search on a phone, often just minutes before someone decides where to go.

Win the moment of decision

When someone searches for a nearby business, they want three things fast: are you open, how far away are you and are you any good? Make those answers impossible to miss.

  • Keep your business profile complete, with accurate hours and photos.
  • Put your address, phone number and hours at the top of your contact page.
  • Show recent reviews where people can see them quickly.

Give people a reason to visit

Search results show who is nearby, but your content explains why someone should choose you. Highlight in-store events, exclusive products, expert advice or services that cannot be delivered online. Update this regularly so returning visitors find something new.

Make the next step easy

Add one-tap calling, clear directions and parking information. If appointments are needed, let people book online. Every extra step between search and visit is an opportunity for someone to choose a competitor instead.

The best local websites feel like a helpful staff member standing at the door.

Track what works

Monitor calls, direction requests and bookings that come from your listings and website. Over time, these numbers show which changes actually bring people in. Our Local SEO services include this kind of reporting as standard.

Is AI Describing Your Business Correctly? A Practical Checklist for 2027

Before many customers ever visit your website, they have already asked an AI assistant about you. They want to know what you do, whether you serve their area and how you compare with the competition. The answer they get shapes their first impression, and it is not always accurate.

The good news is that you have more influence over those answers than you might think. This checklist walks through how to see what AI tools are saying about your business and how to give them better information to work with.

Step 1: Ask the assistants yourself

Start with a simple audit. Ask two or three popular AI assistants the same questions a new customer would ask: "What does your company do?", "Who are the best options for this service near me?" and "What are their hours?" Write down every answer, including the mistakes.

  • Are your services described correctly?
  • Is your location, phone number or service area out of date?
  • Are you mentioned at all when someone asks for recommendations?

Step 2: Fix the facts at the source

AI tools learn from what they can find. If your website, business profiles and directory listings disagree with each other, the assistants will too. Make sure your name, address, phone number, hours and service list match everywhere they appear.

Consistency is the foundation. A single outdated listing can quietly undo a lot of good work.

Step 3: Make your website easy to read

Clear page titles, plain-language service descriptions and structured data help machines understand your site in the same way good design helps people. A dedicated FAQ page that answers real customer questions is one of the most useful additions you can make. If you want help, our AI Search Optimization service covers this end to end.

Step 4: Check again every quarter

AI models are updated constantly, so this is not a one-time task. Repeat your audit every few months, note what has changed and keep your information fresh.

The bottom line

You cannot control every answer an AI assistant gives, but you can make the accurate answer the easiest one for it to find. A few hours of housekeeping now can protect your reputation for years.

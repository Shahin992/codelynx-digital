Why Original Insights Beat Generic Content

The internet is full of articles that say the same thing in slightly different words. With AI tools able to produce that kind of content in seconds, generic writing has never been easier to create or less valuable to publish. What stands out now is content that adds something new.

What counts as original

Original insight does not have to mean groundbreaking research. It means sharing something only your team can share:

  • Lessons from real projects, including what did not work.
  • Data from your own business, even simple numbers.
  • Opinions backed by experience, clearly explained.
  • Step-by-step processes you actually use with clients.

Why it performs better

Search engines and AI systems increasingly look for sources that contribute information others do not. When your article includes details found nowhere else, it becomes worth citing and linking to. Readers notice too, and they are far more likely to trust and contact an expert who clearly knows the subject.

If an article could have been written by anyone, it will be valued by no one.

A simple way to start

Interview someone on your team for fifteen minutes about a recent project. Ask what surprised them and what they would tell a client before starting. Those answers are the raw material for content no competitor can copy.

Make it a habit

Consistency matters more than volume. One genuinely useful article a month will do more for your brand than weekly posts that repeat what everyone else says. Our digital marketing team can help you build a content plan around your expertise.

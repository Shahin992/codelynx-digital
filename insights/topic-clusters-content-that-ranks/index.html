Topic Clusters: Organizing Content That Ranks

Publishing lots of articles is not the same as building authority. Without a plan, content ends up scattered: one post here, another there, none of them clearly connected. Topic clusters bring order to that chaos and help both readers and search engines see the depth of your expertise.

How a topic cluster works

A cluster has two parts. A main "pillar" page covers a broad topic in depth, and several supporting articles each explore one specific question in detail. Every supporting article links back to the pillar, and the pillar links out to each of them.

Why it helps

  • Search engines understand that you cover the topic thoroughly.
  • Readers can easily move from an overview to the detail they need.
  • Your team gets a clear content plan instead of guessing what to write next.

Building your first cluster

Choose one topic closely tied to a service that matters to your business. List the questions customers ask most often about it, then group them. The broad question becomes your pillar page, and each specific question becomes a supporting article.

Think of your content as a library, not a pile of books.

Keep it connected

Internal links are what hold a cluster together, so review them whenever you publish something new. Over time, a few well-built clusters will outperform dozens of unrelated posts. Our digital marketing team can help map out clusters for your business.

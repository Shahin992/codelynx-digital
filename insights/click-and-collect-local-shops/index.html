Click-and-Collect: Helping Local Shops Compete Online

Independent retailers often feel they cannot compete with large online stores. Click-and-collect, where customers buy online and pick up in store, changes that. It combines the convenience of online shopping with the speed and personal touch of a local shop.

Why customers love it

  • No delivery fees or waiting days for a package.
  • Certainty that the item is in stock before they make the trip.
  • A quick, friendly pick-up instead of a crowded checkout line.

Why it works for local shops

Click-and-collect brings people through your door, and many of them buy something extra while they are there. It also helps you compete in online searches, because your products become visible to people looking for them nearby.

What you need to get started

The essentials are simple: an online store with accurate stock levels, a clear pick-up option at checkout and a reliable way to let customers know when their order is ready. Most modern e-commerce platforms support this out of the box.

Accurate inventory is everything. Nothing frustrates a customer like a "ready for pick-up" order that is not actually there.

Make pick-up a great experience

Choose a clearly marked pick-up spot, keep orders organized and greet customers by name. A smooth hand-off turns a convenient transaction into a reason to come back. Our e-commerce team can help you set up click-and-collect the right way.

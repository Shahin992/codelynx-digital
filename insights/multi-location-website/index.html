Planning a Multi-Location Website That Scales

When a business grows to several locations, the website often struggles to keep up. Information gets duplicated, pages fall out of date and customers end up calling the wrong branch. A little planning up front avoids all of that.

Give every location a real page

Each branch deserves its own page with the address, hours, phone number, staff and services offered there. These pages help local customers find the right branch and give search engines a clear signal for each area you serve.

Share what should be shared

Not everything needs to be written separately for each location. Company history, core service descriptions and policies can live in one place and be referenced from every location page. That keeps content consistent and saves your team time.

  • Shared: brand story, service overviews, policies.
  • Location-specific: address, hours, team, reviews, local news.

Make finding a branch easy

A simple location finder, whether a map or a searchable list, lets visitors pick the closest branch in seconds. Combine it with consistent business profiles for each location so the details match everywhere.

Customers should never have to wonder which location they are looking at.

Plan for growth

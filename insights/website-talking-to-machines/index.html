Your Website Is Talking to Machines, Too

For most of the internet's history, websites were built for one audience: people using a browser. That is no longer the whole picture. Search crawlers, AI assistants and automated tools now visit your site constantly, and what they take away shapes how your business is represented elsewhere.

Who these visitors are

Some are familiar, like search engine crawlers that index your pages. Others are newer, such as AI systems that read your content to answer questions. They do not see your design the way a person does. They read the structure, the text and the code underneath.

What they need from you

  • Clear structure: logical headings and well-organized pages.
  • Readable text: important information in actual text, not only inside images.
  • Fast, reliable pages: slow or broken pages may be skipped entirely.
  • Consistent facts: the same details as your other listings across the web.

Why this is a business decision

When machines misunderstand your website, the consequences are real. You might be left out of recommendations, described inaccurately or confused with a competitor. Treating machine readability as part of your digital strategy protects your reputation where you cannot see it directly.

Your website now has two audiences. Design for people, and make sure the machines can follow along.

A practical first step

Ask your web team for a quick review of how your most important pages look without styling or images. If the key message is not clear in plain text, that is where to start. Our digital consulting team can help prioritize the fixes.

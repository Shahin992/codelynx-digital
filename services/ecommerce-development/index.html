Your E-commerce Questions, Answered

How much does an online store cost?

It depends on the platform, the number of products and any custom features. After a short discovery call we provide a detailed, fixed-price proposal.

Which platform is better, Shopify or WooCommerce?

Shopify is a great all-in-one option that is easy to manage. WooCommerce offers more flexibility if you already use WordPress. We will recommend the best fit for your goals.

How long does it take to build an online store?

Most stores take eight to twelve weeks from strategy to launch, depending on catalog size and integrations.

Can you move my existing store to a new platform?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and order history and set up redirects to protect your search rankings.

Digital Consulting FAQs

What is digital strategy consulting?

It is an outside, expert review of how your business uses technology, followed by a clear plan to improve it. We look at your website, software, data and marketing together.

How do you measure success?

We agree on specific metrics up front, such as lead volume, conversion rate or time saved, and track them throughout the engagement.

How long does a strategy engagement take?

Most initial assessments take three to six weeks, depending on the size of your organization and the number of systems involved.

Do you also implement the recommendations?

Yes. Our design and development teams can carry the plan forward, or we can support your internal team or other vendors.

Is consulting only for large companies?

Not at all. Growing businesses often benefit the most, because the right decisions early on prevent costly rework later.

What do we receive at the end?

A written roadmap with priorities, estimated costs, timelines and the metrics we will use to measure progress.

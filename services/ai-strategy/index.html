Real-World Impact: Before & After

Here is how one marketing task changes when a manual process becomes an automated one.

Manual Process (Before)

Total time: about 4 hours per article

  1. ResearchSomeone browses trends and outlines a topic by hand.
  2. DraftingA first draft is written and revised over several rounds.
  3. ImagesStock sites are searched and images resized one by one.
  4. PublishingThe post is formatted, optimized and scheduled manually.

Automated Workflow (After)

Total time: a few minutes of review

  1. Scheduled TriggerA weekly schedule starts the workflow automatically.
  2. Research & DraftingThe agent reviews trends, checks your brand guidelines and writes a search-friendly draft.
  3. Image SuggestionsRelevant images are generated or selected for the article.
  4. Ready for ReviewEverything is saved as a draft for one-click human approval.

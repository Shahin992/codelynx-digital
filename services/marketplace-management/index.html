Your Marketplace Questions, Answered

What is the difference between selling as a vendor and as a seller?

Vendors sell wholesale to the marketplace, while sellers list products directly to customers. We help you choose the model that fits your margins and goals.

How do you optimize product listings?

We research search terms, improve titles and descriptions, upgrade images and add enhanced content that answers shopper questions.

What is enhanced product content?

It is the richer section of a product page with large images, comparison charts and brand storytelling that can lift conversion rates.

Can you help with unauthorized sellers?

Yes. We monitor your listings, document violations and work through the marketplace's brand protection tools to address them.

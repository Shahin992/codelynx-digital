Website Maintenance Questions, Answered

Why do I need a maintenance plan?

Regular updates, backups and monitoring prevent most security issues and outages, and they are far less expensive than emergency repairs.

What happens if my site goes down?

Our monitoring alerts us immediately. We investigate, restore service and let you know what happened and how we prevented it from recurring.

Do your plans include content updates?

Yes. Each plan includes support time for content changes and small improvements.

Can you maintain a site you did not build?

In most cases, yes. We start with a review to make sure the site is in good shape before taking it on.

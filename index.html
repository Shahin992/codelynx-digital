FAQs

Common Questions, Straight Answers

How much does a new website cost?

Every project is different. Price depends on the number of pages, features and integrations you need. After a short discovery call we'll send a detailed quote, or you can try our free website cost calculator for a quick estimate.

How long does the web design process take?

Most websites take six to eight weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger builds can take longer, while quick feedback and ready-to-go content can shorten the timeline.

Will my new website be mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every site we build is fully responsive, so it looks and works great on phones, tablets, laptops and large desktop screens.

Why is local SEO important for my business?

Local SEO helps nearby customers find you at the exact moment they're searching for your services. It's one of the most cost-effective ways to drive calls, visits and qualified leads.

Do you write the content and provide images for my website?

We can. Our copywriters create clear, search-friendly content and we source professional imagery. If you'd rather supply your own, we'll guide you and polish what you have.

What platform do you use to build websites?

We specialize in custom WordPress websites, which give you a flexible, easy-to-update content management system. For online stores we also build on Shopify and WooCommerce.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We regularly modernize outdated sites, improving the design, the user experience and the technical SEO foundation so the new site performs from day one.

What happens after my website is launched?

Launch is the start of the relationship. Our maintenance plans cover updates, backups, security and performance monitoring, and we offer ongoing SEO and marketing to keep your site growing.
